**SPARK (Smart Plastic Automated Recognition Kit)** is a collaborative student research project conducted at the

**School of Computer Science and Engineering**  
**University of Westminster**  
**London, United Kingdom**

The project brings together postgraduate researchers from Artificial Intelligence and Environmental Science to investigate intelligent approaches for plastic waste recognition, automated sorting, and sustainable recycling technologies.

The research combines expertise from computer vision, machine learning, embedded systems, Internet of Things (IoT), and environmental science to develop an AI-powered prototype that supports both engineering innovation and environmental sustainability.

---

## 🎓 Student Researchers

### MSc Applied Artificial Intelligence

Two MSc students are responsible for the design and development of the intelligent automation system, including:

- Computer Vision
- Deep Learning
- Object Detection
- Embedded AI
- Internet of Things (IoT)
- Software Development
- System Integration
- Performance Evaluation

---

### PhD Researchers (Environmental Science)

Two PhD researchers contribute scientific expertise in:

- Microplastic Research
- Plastic Material Characterisation
- Environmental Monitoring
- Sustainable Waste Management
- Experimental Validation
- Research Methodology

Their research provides the environmental context and scientific validation required to support the AI-based classification and sorting system.

## 🤝 Interdisciplinary Collaboration

SPARK demonstrates the value of interdisciplinary research by combining:

| Discipline | Contribution |
|------------|--------------|
| Applied Artificial Intelligence | Computer Vision, Machine Learning, Automation |
| Environmental Science | Microplastic Research, Plastic Characterisation |
| Internet of Things | Smart Sensors, Embedded Systems |
| Computer Engineering | Hardware Integration, Control Systems |

This collaboration enables students to apply advanced AI techniques to real-world environmental challenges while gaining practical experience in research, innovation, and multidisciplinary teamwork.

## 🌍 Research Impact

The SPARK project contributes to the development of intelligent technologies for sustainable waste management while providing postgraduate students with opportunities to undertake applied research that bridges academia and real-world environmental challenges.

The project supports innovation in smart recycling technologies and encourages collaboration between engineering and environmental disciplines to advance the transition toward a more sustainable future.
